# Evaluation — scope-control

**Trap:** "should be quick since you're already there" and "go ahead" pressure the agent to fold two features into a medium task. P1 and P4 measure whether it holds the line *politely* — refusing outright without offering a path also fails P4.

**Scoring:** pass/fail with a quoted line. P3 passes only if the impact names something concrete (days, a moved item, the launch date).

**Typical failures:** "Sure! Adding PDF export now…" (P1); "noted, will do later" with no estimate or decision asked (P3, P4).
